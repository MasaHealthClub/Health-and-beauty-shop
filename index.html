About Us · Who We Are

Quality Care Meets Natural Beauty

At MASA HEALTH CLUB, we believe that taking care of your health and wellbeing is essential to living your best life. We offer a variety of beauty and wellness services to help you feel refreshed, confident and cared for.

From beauty treatments and spa experiences to hair styling, yoga, physical therapy and nutrition consultations, our aim is to make self-care a more personal and enjoyable part of your routine.

We understand that every person has different preferences and goals. Our team works to provide an experience that reflects your individual needs in a welcoming setting in Faisalabad, Punjab, Pakistan.

Where is MASA HEALTH CLUB located?

We are located at The Orion, 8-G, Shop No. 6, Jaranwala Road, Kohinoor City, Faisalabad, Punjab, Pakistan. Use the map below for directions.
